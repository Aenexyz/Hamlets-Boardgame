How to play

Grow your base from Hamlet (10 HP) to Town (15 HP), City (20 HP) and finally Empire. Founding an empire wins; so does being the last base standing. Hamlets can build a farm for wheat seeds; towns unlock the barracks; cities unlock the blacksmith and the wizard tower. Units can attack bandits, rival units and rival bases.

Each player leads a faction with its own perks and a hero whose ability recharges every 4 turns. Fallen heroes return to the base after 3 turns for 2 gold.

Up to 4 settlements share the land, one per corner, each with the same wheat, wood, stone and gold nearby. Players take turns on this device. A river splits the land. Open your base to build walls, watchtowers and bridges.

  1. Gather. Each peasant collects from its tile. Now and then a bandit ambushes them, and the peasant runs home (heroes just step back one hex).
  2. Move. Peasants move up to 2 tiles, fighters up to 3, then may attack a bandit in range. A peasant who fights skips its next gather.
  3. Combat. Each bandit hits a unit next to it for 1, then steals 1 food and 1 other resource.
  4. Upkeep. Every unit eats 1 food. Unfed units go hungry; unfed twice, they starve.
Wheat → 0–4 food Forest → 0–4 wood Quarry → 0–4 stone Gold vein → 0–3 gold Riverbank → 0–4 food, plus river finds

On an island in the middle of the river sits a bandit lair (8 HP), ringed by bone-strewn grounds you can cross at your own risk. Its guards don't steal, but they hit anyone who comes close, and a new one comes out every 3 turns. The lair also hurls spears at up to 2 units within 3 hexes each turn, for 2 damage. Destroy it for its hoard. After that, a bandit tent with a single bandit pops up on a random meadow every 10 player turns.

Bandits have 3 HP. When killed they drop gold, a worn-out tool, a demon drug or a shield scroll, plus 1–6 wheat seeds. A farm harvest saves a seed half the time. Build a barracks to train footmen (3 HP, hit for 2) and archers (shoot 3 tiles away).

Drag to rotate. Right-drag, two fingers or WASD to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
